## Recipes

The bundled catalog (`data/recipes.json`, ids 910001 to 910010) holds the ten dishes from the design. Each dish links
to the recipe that inspired it ("Recipe by …" on the recipe screen, from `sourceName` / `sourceUrl`). The ingredient
lists, steps and summaries are written in our own words for Sous, and the nutrition values are our own per-serving
estimates, not the sources' numbers. Beef & Rice Skillet follows the cooking-mode flow in the Figma design (an
oven-finished soy, beef and green onion skillet), so it differs from the linked version.

Recipes fetched live from Spoonacular keep their own `sourceName` / `sourceUrl` next to a backlink to
[spoonacular.com/food-api](https://spoonacular.com/food-api), as the Spoonacular API terms require.
